ARTIFACTS

Put pictures or animated GIFs in this folder and FalseHorizons floats them around on top of the visuals: they drift, tilt a little, swell with the
bass and jump on a beat, and the visuals smear them as they go. Pictures with see-through parts (PNG, GIF) work best. Up to 12 are shown.

Formats: JPEG, PNG, WebP, BMP, GIF (animated too), TGA, PSD, PIC, PNM.
Take a picture out of the folder to stop using it. The program notices new and removed files by itself; no restart needed.
With nothing here there are no artifacts, just the visuals.
